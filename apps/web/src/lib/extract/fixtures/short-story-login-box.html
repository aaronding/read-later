Harbourside Notes

Library opens a tool lending shelf

The central library has opened a shelf of tools that card holders can borrow for a week at a time, from drills and ladders to a pressure washer and a sewing machine. Staff say the idea came from readers who kept asking whether the library could lend more than books.

The first hundred items were donated by a hardware shop that closed last spring, and volunteers from the repair café check each tool when it comes back. Anything that needs a blade or a battery is serviced every month.

Borrowing is free, though the library asks for a deposit on the larger items. A waiting list for the tile cutter already runs into December.

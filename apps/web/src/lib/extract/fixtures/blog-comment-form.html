Harbour Photo Diary

Photos from a small harbour town, most mornings.

Harbour fog

The fog came in just after six and sat on the water until the first ferry pushed through it. From the end of the breakwater the boats at their moorings were only shapes, and the lighthouse kept sounding every half minute.

By eight it had lifted from the town but not from the bay, so the fishing boats came home out of a white wall, one after another, with their lights still on. The gulls followed them in.

These were taken with the small camera, without a tripod, which is why the last two are a little soft. Tomorrow's forecast is clear, so the next set should be sharper, and there may be one of the ferry leaving at first light.

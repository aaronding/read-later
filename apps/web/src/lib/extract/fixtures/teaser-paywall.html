Why the city's tram plan stalled

When the city first proposed a tram line along the old canal route, the plan had support from almost every party on the council. Eight years later, not a single rail has been laid, and the project's budget has been revised four times.

Officials blame rising construction costs and a long dispute over the route through the market district. Business owners there say they were never properly consulted, and that years of roadworks would have driven away the customers they depend on.

Supporters of the scheme say the delays have already cost more than the disruption would have.

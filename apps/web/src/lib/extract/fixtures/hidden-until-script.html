The night market moves to the harbour

The night market that has filled Station Street every Friday for twenty years will move to the old harbour car park in the spring, the council said on Tuesday. Stallholders had asked for more room and for shelter from the wind that comes down the street from the hills.

The car park holds twice as many stalls, and the council will put up a roof over the food stalls and lights along the quay. Buses will run later on market nights, and the ferry will stop at the harbour on its last crossing so that people from the islands can come over.

Some shopkeepers on Station Street worry that they will lose the evening trade the market brings. The council says it will try a smaller craft market there on the first Friday of each month and look again at the numbers after a year.
